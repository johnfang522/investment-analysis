Investment Thesis

NVIDIA is the toll road of the Artificial Intelligence (AI) build-out, and the toll is still rising: Q2 FY27 revenue of $96.22B grew +105.9% Year-over-Year (YoY) at a 66.2% operating margin, and the Q3 guide of $108.0B (±2%) sits above the $104.6B Street estimate. The anchor metric is the ~14.7x forward Price-to-Earnings (P/E) on a ~$15.70 FY28 consensus Earnings Per Share (EPS): optically cheap for a Rule-of-40 score of 131. What keeps conviction at 6 rather than 8 is that the growth is increasingly financed by NVIDIA itself — Q2 Free Cash Flow (FCF) covered only 0.36x of net income on a $22.3B receivables build, net cash halved to ~$23.2B from $45.1B a year ago, and $108.5B of guarantees plus customer equity stakes tie the downside to the AI cycle. We own it, but as a starter bought on a pullback, not a core position chased near the 52-week high.
