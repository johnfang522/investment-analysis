Knockout Gate

Gate: FLAG

  • Nov 23 update: Huang's reinstatement settles who runs the company but not the integrity question. That question has widened: the SEC is reportedly probing Nvidia's accounting (Reuters via Investing.com, Nov 20, 2026), on top of the open board investigation and the federal Megaspeed probe (CNBC, Nov 23, 2026).
  • Why FLAG and not PASS: the skill's disqualifiers include undisclosed related-party dealings and accounting manipulation. Both are now under live investigation, and neither has been cleared. The government's 'no national security risk' assurance concerns Huang personally, not the accounting or export compliance.
  • Why FLAG and not FAIL: Points 14 and 15 score 1, not 0. There is no finding, charge, restatement or confirmed export breach. Reuters notes investigations 'are not evidence of wrongdoing', and Nvidia's 2002 SEC accounting matter ended in a settlement without admission.
  • Effect: the FLAG caps the classification at Watchlist (29/40 would otherwise be 'Strong candidate'). The call is PASS until the gate resolves.
  • Back to PASS: the independent committee's findings are published and clear information security and export compliance, and Nvidia discloses the scope of the SEC inquiry or it closes. The House interviews (Dec 1–2) and the Q3 10-Q legal-proceedings note are the next checkpoints.
  • To FAIL: a restatement or SEC finding on revenue, guarantees or customer financing; evidence Nvidia staff knew of Megaspeed diversion; or evidence the CEO concealed the relationship from the board while Megaspeed was a customer.
  • Unchanged: dilution and runway checks still pass easily (share count down, $227.3B buyback authorization, Q4 guide $130.0B).

Sell Rules and Add-More Triggers

  • 1. Mistake in the analysis: facts that, if false, break the thesis:
    • FY2028 revenue growth falls well short of the ~70% guide for demand reasons rather than supply.
    • A top-four customer moves frontier training (not just inference) onto custom silicon.
    • Gross margin settles below 70% rather than the guided 72–73% for FY2028.
  • 2. Business deterioration: measurable signals:
    • Governance and accounting: the board investigation finds compromised information or an export-control lapse; or the SEC probe produces a restatement or finding on revenue, guarantees or customer financing. That is Fisher's integrity sell: exit, regardless of price.
    • Operating cash flow below 0.6x net income for two quarters (Q2 FY2027: 0.40x; Q3 FCF recovered to 0.67x), or DSO above 70 days (now 64).
    • Any guarantee called, impairment on AI-cloud commitments, or write-down of a strategic equity stake.
    • Largest customer above 25% of revenue (22% in Q4 FY2026).
    • Huang steps back again without a named successor, or the CFO departs.
