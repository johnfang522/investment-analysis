Business & Competitive Position

  • What it does: designs, makes and sells the da Vinci and Ion robotic surgery systems and, more importantly, the instruments, accessories and services that run on them — ~11,710 da Vinci systems installed (+12%), Q2 2026 procedures ~889K (+15%), and instruments & accessories ~60% of Q2 revenue (Intuitive Q2 2026 release).
  • Moat: a razor-and-blade installed base with high switching costs (surgeon training, hospital workflow, 20+ years of clinical data) generates recurring revenue of roughly 85% of the total and funds $1.31B of annual research and development (R&D, +15%, FY2025 10-K).
  • Key risk to the moat: well-funded rivals (Medtronic's Hugo, J&J's Ottava, others) winning meaningful U.S. soft-tissue placements; this cannot show up in procedure data before about 2028, and regulatory timelines conflict across sources.
