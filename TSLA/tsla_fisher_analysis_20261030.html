Scuttlebutt verdict: MIXED

  • Customers are the strongest channel. Loyalty leads the US industry, and FSD attach is high. The product franchise is real.
  • Competitive standing is slipping on both fronts. Chinese brands are taking share in EV volume, and Waymo leads driverless rides by an order of magnitude.
  • Regulators and the crash data are a live risk. An NHTSA engineering analysis is one step from a 3.2M-vehicle recall.
  • Management candor is the weakest channel. Each new autonomy date replaces the last missed one, and that pattern drives the Point 14–15 scores below.

Knockout Gate

Gate: FAIL

  • Point 15 scores 0, which is an automatic FAIL under the gate rules.
  • Repeated broken promises: unsupervised driving was promised for 2017, 2020, 2025 and Q4 2026, and is now Q2 2027 (Electrek tracker; Q3 2026 call). This is a pattern, not one slip.
  • Related-party drift: the dealings are disclosed, so this is not concealment. But Terafab has no binding terms, IP rights or cost split, per SpaceX's own S-1, while Tesla spends on it (Electrek, May 20, 2026). Long-term cost is guided at over $100B (Q3 2026 call).
  • Point 13 at 0 does not trip the dilution rule: growth is cash-funded. The dilution is compensation-driven, which is a governance cost, not a financing need.
  • Per the skill rules, a FAIL ends the investment case. The rest of the report shows why the score and price would not rescue it anyway.

Sell Rules and Add-More Triggers

  • 1. Mistake in the analysis: for a holder, these facts would break a long thesis:
    • Unsupervised FSD is not on customer cars by end-2027 (now guided Q2 2027).
    • Robotaxi shows no 'noticeable financial impact' by Q2 2027, as Musk guided on the Q3 2026 call.
    • The 33.1% energy gross margin proves to be mostly tariff-refund one-offs.
  • 2. Business deterioration: measurable signals:
    • Operating margin below 3% for two more quarters (TTM 3.3%; Q3 2.6%).
    • Negative FCF for FY2026 or a 2027 capex guide above $25B without a funding plan.
    • An NHTSA recall from the FSD engineering analysis, or the departure of the AI/autonomy lead.
    • Any new related-party transaction with SpaceX or xAI not put to disinterested shareholders.
  • 3. Clearly better opportunity: the bar a replacement must clear:
    • Any compounder with gate PASS, ROIC above its cost of capital, and a base-case return above 10%/yr. Nearly any Fisher-grade name clears the +3.1% base case here.
    • Under Fisher, a rising price or high valuation alone is not a sell signal. But at 227.1x the expected return is already near zero, which argues for minimal exposure.
  • What would reopen the case (add-more triggers do not apply to an AVOID):
    • Gate repair first: Terafab and SpaceX terms disclosed, priced at arm's length and approved by disinterested shareholders, plus two consecutive autonomy milestones delivered on the stated date.
    • Then price: the base case earns 10%/yr from about $292, a market-driven drop below the 52-week low ($297.38) with fundamentals unchanged.

Key Risks and What to Monitor

  • Risk to our AVOID call: autonomy works. If unsupervised FSD ships in Q2 2027 and Robotaxi scales across cities, the bull case (+26%/yr) is open, and Fisher's 'never sell a great company' logic would have been wrong to apply here.
  • Key-person and governance: the thesis, the pay plan and the related-party web all run through one person who also runs SpaceX.
  • Capex without returns: ~$20B in 2026 plus Terafab at 'tens of billions' through early 2028 and >$100B long-term, with ROIC already ~5%.
  • Competition on two fronts: Chinese EV makers on price and volume (BYD ahead in Europe), Waymo on driverless scale (500K+ paid rides/week).
  • Regulatory: the NHTSA FSD engineering analysis (3.2M vehicles) and growing Robotaxi incident counts.
