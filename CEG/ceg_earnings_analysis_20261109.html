6. Headwinds / Red Flags

  • PJM intervention: the January 2026 governors' plan and PJM's request to extend capacity price collars to the 2028/29 and 2029/30 auctions cap the merchant upside that drove the 2024–25 re-rating.
  • Co-location rules: FERC's ruling (1H 2027) decides how front-of-meter vs behind-the-meter hyperscaler deals are priced and who pays transmission.
  • Customer concentration: the incremental growth story rests on a handful of hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft/Crane, Meta); a capex pullback would hit new-deal pricing first.
  • Non-GAAP creep: Calpine integration ($73M) and acquired-contract amortization ($39M) are new recurring add-backs this year; the −$108M tax-apportionment item is lumpy.
  • Assets held for sale ($5.66B): DOJ-required divestitures will shrink Calpine's contribution once closed.
