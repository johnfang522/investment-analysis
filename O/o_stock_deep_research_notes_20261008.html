Investment Thesis

Realty Income is the best-in-class net-lease Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT): 98.8% occupancy, A-category credit (Moody's A3 / S&P A− / Fitch A), net debt of 5.4x annualized adjusted Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization and Rent (EBITDAre), and a covered 6.0% dividend ($3.26, about 73% of Adjusted Funds From Operations, AFFO). The problem is the return engine: revenue grows +9.7% year-over-year (YoY) but AFFO per share only +3.8% ($1.09 in Q2) because shares rose 3.5% and new debt costs more than the 3.9% legacy book, so FY26 guidance of $4.44–$4.45 is only ~+4%. At 12.2x FY26 AFFO the stock is fairly priced for that, and its price is set by the 10-year Treasury (5.23% today, up from 4.79% on Sept 2), which has compressed the dividend spread to +77 basis points (bp) from +123 bp. Price is at its 52-week closing low in a confirmed downtrend. A great income asset, no margin of safety on rates: PASS.
