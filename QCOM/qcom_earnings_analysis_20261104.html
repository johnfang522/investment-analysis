1. Headline Quality

  • The revenue beat is operational: auto ($1.99B vs $1.66B est.) and IoT ($1.88B, slightly above) drove it. Handsets ($5.14B vs $5.21B est.) and Qualcomm Technology Licensing (QTL, $1.30B, in line) did not.
  • The EPS beat is thin: $2.17 vs $2.16, at a fixed 12.5% non-GAAP tax rate that includes a foreign-currency benefit on the Korean withholding-tax receivable. Non-GAAP EBT fell 30% YoY.
  • Guide shape is the tell: the FQ1'27 revenue midpoint ($10.8B) is ~3% above consensus, but the EPS midpoint ($2.25) is below it. More revenue at lower margin.
  • Non-GAAP revenue add-back: the $120M legal-settlement payment to a customer is a contra-revenue item excluded from non-GAAP. Without it the beat still holds ($10.32B vs ~$10.12B).

Risk Headwinds

  • 🔴 Margin compression is partly structural: four straight gross-margin declines to 50.7% (GAAP), with ASIC/data-center mix permanently dilutive (~3 points to QCT).

  • 🔴 Inventory +32% vs revenue −8%: DIO ~155 days. Strategic buffer or channel risk; either way it consumed $2.05B of FY26 cash.
  • ⚠️ Rising SBC: FQ1'27 SBC at $0.99/share vs $0.72. GAAP earnings power is roughly half the headline.
  • ⚠️ Returns outrunning FCF: $11.67B returned vs $8.65B FCF; the buyback pace is already slowing.
