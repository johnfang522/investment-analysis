Investment Thesis

Qualcomm is a high-quality licensing-plus-silicon franchise in a handset core that is shrinking, priced as if the diversification story were already working. Handsets were $5.1B, 51% of fiscal Q3 revenue, and fell 20% YoY, which pulled operating margin down 10.3 points to 16.3% on a 4.0% revenue decline [SEC EDGAR; Qualcomm release 7/29/2026]. Automotive (+61% to $1.6B) is real, but data center, the largest driver in our growth bridge (+$9.0B by FY+5), has no disclosed customer revenue. The shareholder-return engine has also outrun cash generation: 9M fiscal 2026 payouts of $9.67B exceeded free cash flow (FCF) of $6.83B, and net cash has become $6.97B of net debt [Qualcomm 10-Q / 8-K]. Our probability-weighted fiscal 2031 (FY+5) value of $216 equals only +4.0% per year from $178.40, below a 10–12% hurdle, so we are PASS.
