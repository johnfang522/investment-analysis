0. Theme Summary

A broad scan of the AI build-out finds the binding constraint moving down the stack. Power equipment is now consensus (GE Vernova gas-turbine backlog plus slot reservations of 116 GW, trade reports of deliveries running to 2031) and memory is a recognized shortage, so the least-owned physical chokepoint is the indium phosphide (InP) laser inside every 800G and 1.6T optical link: Lumentum's CEO says it is shipping more than 30% below customer demand and capacity is allocated under long-term agreements through 2027. The structural case is strong, but the entry has changed this week. The AI-financing scare around CoreWeave (bonds at about 15% yield) took the optical leaders down 25% in five sessions, even though they are still up YTD. The opportunity is therefore a scaled, financing-aware position in the laser chokepoint, not a chase of the whole AI complex.
