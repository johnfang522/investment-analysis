3. Growth Durability

  • Engine: SI solutions & infrastructure revenue $7.02B (vs $2.19B in Q2 and $0.36B a year ago). Management says ex-Cursor it grew 192% QoQ, driven by new cloud services agreements and Grok adoption.
  • Visibility: backlog $63B at Sept 30 (vs $47.5B at Q2), of which $17.5B is deferred revenue and ~$42B should be recognized within a year. $42B over four quarters is ~$10.5B/quarter — below Q3's $14.6B run-rate, so the Q1 2027 guide of $33B–$37B needs further signings or conversion beyond booked backlog.
  • Year-end target: $100B annualized (December) = ~$8.3B per month vs Q3's average of ~$4.9B per month, i.e. ~+71% from the Q3 average.
  • Connectivity: 13.6M consumer subscribers (+1.6M QoQ), ARPU $69 (+5% QoQ), enterprise & government revenue +91% YoY. Healthy and decelerating from the SI-driven hypergrowth only in relative terms (+22% QoQ).
  • Compute: nameplate 1.7 GW (1.4 GW in Q2); management targets ~3 GW by year-end and 13 GW by end of 2027 (call). Capacity delivery, not demand, is the stated constraint.
  • Concentration caveat: one independent analysis estimates ~75% of Q3 SI revenue came from short-term, spot-priced GPU rentals to a few AI labs. We could not verify that from the release; treat it as unconfirmed.

Bright Spots

  • Backlog of $63B (+$15.5B QoQ) and a Q1 2027 revenue guide of $33B–$37B — the most durable signal in the print.
  • Connectivity is a profitable compounding asset: operating income $2.37B (+99% YoY), Adjusted EBITDA margin 66.7%, 13.6M consumer subscribers and ARPU rising to $69.
  • SI turned operating-profitable at $431M even after $1.2B of acquisition SBC (~$1.6B excluding it, per management).
  • Operating leverage is real: incremental operating margin ~43% and operating income up $2.9B QoQ.
  • Balance-sheet capacity: $89.9B of cash and securities and $47.7B net cash fund the next two quarters of the build.

Risk Headwinds

  • Cash burn is accelerating: FCF ≈ −$10.0B in Q3 and a Q4 capex guide of $35B–$40B point to a −$20B to −$30B quarter (our estimate).
  • SI revenue quality is unproven: contract terms and customer concentration undisclosed; one analyst estimate (unverified) puts spot-priced GPU rental at ~75% of Q3 SI revenue.
  • Margin gains partly one-time: management itself guides Q4 gross margin back toward Q2 (55.3%) after customer-mix and tariff-refund benefits.
  • Earnings flattered below the line: $1.4B of other income and tax benefit; $561M of the other income is related-party.
  • SBC drag of ~$2B per quarter from Cursor and rising non-GAAP add-backs; Tesla combination and lock-up supply add event risk.

Net Verdict

Net score +3 → Constructive. The quarter is a genuine operating inflection — SI went profitable, Connectivity expanded margins, and backlog plus a doubling revenue guide give unusual visibility — but it is financed by cash burn that is about to roughly double. The stock's modest after-hours move after a ~14% pre-print slide looks proportionate to that mix. Earnings quality supports the franchise; it does not by itself reverse our Oct 8 PASS, which rested on valuation and the capex-return debate, and the revenue base under that valuation now needs to be redone.
