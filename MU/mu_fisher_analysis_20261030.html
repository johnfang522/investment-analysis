  • Justification: Micron is executing superbly: FY2026 revenue $133.19B (+256%), an 80.7% operating margin and ~$38B of net cash. But Fisher's framework is about compounding through cycles, and memory does not compound. It has swung from −$5.34 to $74.33 of EPS in three years. At 24/40 (Watchlist), with the weakest HBM position of the three DRAM makers and an industry capex wave landing in late 2028, the 5.1x multiple is a peak multiple on peak earnings. Normalizing EPS ~12%/yr lower over five years gives +4.4%/yr.
  • Price: demanding once earnings are normalized; the low P/E is the warning sign, not the opportunity.
  • What would change the call: two years of stable DRAM pricing after the 2028 capacity arrives (proof of a structural business), or a price below ~$556.
