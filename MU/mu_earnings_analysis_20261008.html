  • HBM is the mix engine: HBM4 revenue passed $1B, HBM grew faster than the company, and the Cloud Memory unit earned an 83% gross margin on $16.28B.
  • No inventory build: inventory was $8.57B at fiscal Q3 versus $8.36B at fiscal 2025 year-end while revenue is ~4x, and days sales outstanding held at ~59 — scarcity, not channel stuffing.

Risk Headwinds

  • Capex doubling is the structural risk: ~$25B in the first half of fiscal 2027 and more in the second half, against $27.4B for all of fiscal 2026; new capacity lands in 2028 — the classic memory-cycle supply response (Bloomberg Law; Herald, Sep 30–Oct 1, 2026).
  • Price exposure remains: analysts estimate ~60% of production is outside fixed-price contracts (Morgan Stanley via Parameter), so a DRAM price turn hits earnings even with high contracted volume.
  • Margin gains have stalled: Q1 gross margin is guided down ~75bp from 87.0%; with margin near 87%, the skew from here is flat-to-down.
  • Data gap on the balance sheet: fiscal Q4 inventory and receivables were not retrievable, so the no-build read relies on fiscal Q3.
