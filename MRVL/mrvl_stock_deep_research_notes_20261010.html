Cash conversion is the weak link — SBC of $326.2M absorbed two-thirds of Q2 free cash flow, leaving only $152.6M after SBC. Great company, fully priced stock: we PASS at spot and would start a position inside $236–$258.

Business & Competitive Position

  • What it does: designs data-infrastructure semiconductors — optical interconnect (electro-optics, 1.6T optical digital signal processors), custom accelerators (XPUs) for hyperscalers and switching — with Data Center at 79% of revenue, +46% YoY (Q2 FY2027 release, Aug 27, 2026).
  • Moat: a narrow-to-wide moat in optical interconnect (design wins and qualification cycles at hyperscalers, scale in mixed-signal and packaging) and an installed position in multi-year custom programs; the Celestial AI acquisition (~24.6M shares issued) extends it into photonic fabric.
  • Key risk to the moat: hyperscalers moving custom silicon sockets to Broadcom or Alchip — custom is $12.0B (36%) of our FY2031 base-case revenue and is where share is contested.
