Coherent is a real artificial-intelligence (AI) optics winner — revenue grew 33.7% year-over-year (YoY) to $2.05B in the June 2026 quarter, Datacenter & Communications is 79% of sales and growing 58.6%, and scarce indium-phosphide (InP) laser capacity plus a $2B Nvidia stake and supply agreement make it a structural share gainer (SEC EDGAR; Coherent 8-K, Aug 2026). But at $288.02 the market cap is $56.72B, or 7.9x trailing sales and 30.6x FY2027E non-GAAP EPS of $9.42, and the stock already discounts FY2028E EPS of $14.05. Earnings are being capitalised before they are converted to cash, and our probability-weighted FY+5 value of $356 returns only 4.6% a year from here. We pass at spot and want either a lower price or proof that the working-capital build turns into cash.

Business & Competitive Position

  • What it does: Coherent designs and manufactures engineered materials, lasers and optical transceivers; Datacenter & Communications (800G and 1.6T transceivers, InP lasers) is 79% of revenue, up 58.6% YoY (Coherent 8-K, Aug 2026).
  • Moat: vertical integration — it self-supplies InP and VCSEL (vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser) lasers, the scarce input in the optics shortage, and is the scaled US-sourced option if China-based optics are restricted; the $2B Nvidia stake and supply agreement adds a demand anchor.
  • Key risk to the moat: co-packaged optics (CPO) moving the optical engine in-house at Nvidia or Broadcom, or Chinese 1.6T pricing compressing Datacenter & Communications gross margin (business-overview appendix).
